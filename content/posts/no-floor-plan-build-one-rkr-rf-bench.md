What do you do if you don't have a floor plan? You just build one.

That is the starting point for the floor plan builder in RKR RF Bench. You answer a few questions about the building, or you draw the rooms yourself. You can change the wall materials, place access points, and watch the coverage show up on the plan. If you prefer a head start, open a template and edit from there.

The short video below walks through that path on an office floor, then again on a student dorm template.

Watch it on YouTube: https://youtu.be/hnKmeA0mp7A

## More than the floor plan

RKR RF Bench is a place to learn RF and Wi-Fi in the browser. Alongside the floor plan builder you will find Circuit, Antenna, Test, Wi-Fi 7 AP, and SoC. There are lessons under Learn, and a Knowledge base when you want the deeper read. How far you go is up to you.
