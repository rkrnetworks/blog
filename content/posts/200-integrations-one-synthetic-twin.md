RKR SETU now has 200+ integrations.

Every estate is a mix. Switches and access points from different years. Cloud networks next to racks in the basement. Sensors and building systems, each with its own tool. Security and IT systems that each hold one part of the picture.

RKR SETU integrates read-only by default with 200+ platforms across network, cloud, compute, IoT, buildings, security and IT, and brings what they hold into the [synthetic twin](/glossary/#synthetic-twin). You get one model of the estate, not seven dashboards.

## What the 200+ cover

- **Network:** switching, routing, wireless and the tools that manage them.
- **Cloud:** cloud networks next to the on-site estate.
- **Compute:** servers and storage.
- **IoT:** sensors and other devices on the network.
- **Buildings:** building management systems, building data and energy metering.
- **Security:** the security tools already in the estate.
- **IT:** asset, IPAM, DCIM and CMDB records.

See the full list on the [integrations page](https://www.rkr-networks.com/integrations).

## Why one model matters

Most outages and bad changes aren't hard to fix. They're hard to see coming, because the answer is split across systems that don't know about each other.

When all of it sits in one synthetic twin, you can ask the questions that cross those lines. What runs in this rack? Who loses service if this switch fails? What does this change touch? Ask in the model first, before you touch the real estate.

That's the idea behind the video: 200+ integrations, one synthetic twin.
